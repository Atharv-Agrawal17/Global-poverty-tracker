What Does This Measure Mean?

The main international indicator uses the World Bank's international poverty line of $3.00 per person per day at 2021 PPP.

This measure is intended for international comparisons and is different from each country's national poverty line.

Countries do not necessarily have observations for the same year. The dashboard therefore displays the actual year associated with each available observation.

Data Sources and Methodology

Primary source: World Bank Poverty and Inequality Platform (PIP).

The dashboard is designed to retrieve poverty statistics from the World Bank rather than manually entering country values.

Poverty observations are not available for the same years in every country. Missing data is therefore displayed as unavailable.

Always consider the observation year when comparing countries.
